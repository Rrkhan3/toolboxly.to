Case Converter

Use this free online case converter to change text case instantly. It works right in your browser, with no sign-up.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server.

Advertisement

How to use the Case Converter

  1. Type or paste your text into the box above.
  2. Press the action button. The Word and Character Counters update live as you type.
  3. Copy or download the result, or press Clear to start again.

How it works

The selected rule is applied to your text. camelCase, snake_case and kebab-case first split the text into words.

Example

"hello world" becomes "Hello World" in Title Case and "hello_world" in snake_case.

Case Converter FAQ

What does sentence case do?

It capitalizes the first letter of each sentence and lowercases the rest.

Are line breaks kept?

Yes for upper, lower, title and sentence case. Code-style cases join everything into one identifier.

Is the Case Converter free to use?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and runs in your browser.
