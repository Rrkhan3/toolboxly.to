Text Cleaner

Remove extra spaces, line breaks and unwanted characters from text. Free online text cleaner for cleaner content.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server.

How to use the Text Cleaner

  1. Paste the messy text into the input.
  2. Run the cleaner to strip extra spaces or line breaks as offered.
  3. Copy the cleaned output.
  4. Repeat with new text as needed.

How it works

The cleaner applies rules such as collapsing repeated spaces, trimming edges and normalizing line breaks so text becomes easier to paste into documents or forms.

Helpful after copying from PDFs, emails or web pages.

Examples

  • “Hello   world” → “Hello world”.
  • Multiple blank lines reduced according to the tool’s settings.
  • Leading and trailing spaces removed.

Text Cleaner FAQ

Does it change my words?

No. It targets whitespace and formatting noise, not spelling.

Is text sent to a server?

Cleaning runs in your browser for this tool.

Is the Text Cleaner free?

Yes.
