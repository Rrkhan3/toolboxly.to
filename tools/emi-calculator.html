Free EMI Calculator – Monthly Loan Payment

Plan loans with this EMI calculator: monthly installment from principal, annual rate and tenure. Useful for personal loans, vehicle finance and rough mortgage estimates. Math runs in the browser so loan figures are not submitted to a server by the tool.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server by this tool.

How to use the EMI Calculator

Enter loan amount, annual interest rate and tenure in months or years. The calculator shows monthly EMI plus total interest and total payment.

How it works

Uses the standard reducing-balance EMI formula: EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), where r is the monthly rate and n is the number of months.

Why use the EMI Calculator

Plan home, car or personal loan affordability before you commit—without a bank visit or spreadsheet.

Common use cases

  • Home and auto loan planning
  • Personal loan comparison
  • Teaching amortization basics
  • Budgeting monthly obligations

EMI Calculator FAQ

Is this the same as bank EMI?

It matches the common mathematical formula; banks may add fees or use different day-count conventions.

Can I change tenure unit?

Use the fields as labeled (years/months depending on the form).

Is the EMI Calculator free?

Yes.

Tips for best results

Rates and fees vary by lender; treat results as estimates. Confirm tenure units (months vs years) match the form fields. This is not financial advice.
