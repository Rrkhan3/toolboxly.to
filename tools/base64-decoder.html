Base64 Decoder

Use this free online base64 decoder to decode Base64 to text. It works right in your browser, with no sign-up.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server.

Advertisement

How to use the Base64 Decoder

  1. Paste or type your code or text into the input editor.
  2. Press the action button (Format, Validate, Encode, Decode or Generate).
  3. Copy or download the output. HTML, CSS and JavaScript tools also offer a sandboxed Preview tab.

How it works

The Base64 text is converted back to bytes and read as UTF-8 text.

Example

"SGVsbG8=" becomes "Hello".

Base64 Decoder FAQ

Why does decoding fail?

The input may contain characters outside A–Z, a–z, 0–9, + / = or may not decode to UTF-8 text. URL-safe characters (- and _) are accepted.

Can it decode files?

No. This tool decodes text only.

Is the Base64 Decoder free to use?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and runs in your browser.
