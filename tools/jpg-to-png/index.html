JPG to PNG Converter

Convert JPG/JPEG images to PNG format online. Free browser-based converter with local processing.

Your image is processed locally in your browser and is never uploaded.

How to use the JPG to PNG Converter

  1. Select a JPG/JPEG file.
  2. Convert to PNG.
  3. Download the PNG output.
  4. Repeat for additional images as needed.

How it works

The image is decoded and re-encoded as PNG in the browser. PNG supports lossless compression and transparency (though a typical JPG source has no alpha channel).

Use when you need PNG for editing pipelines or platforms that prefer it.

Examples

  • photo.jpg → photo.png with the same visual content.
  • File size may increase because PNG is often larger for photographs.
  • Transparency is not created automatically from a standard JPG.

JPG to PNG Converter FAQ

Will quality improve?

Converting JPG to PNG does not restore detail lost in the original JPG compression.

Is conversion done locally?

Yes, in your browser for this tool.

Is the JPG to PNG Converter free?

Yes.
