Length Converter

Convert length units: meters, feet, inches, kilometers, miles and more. Free online length converter tool.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server.

How to use the Length Converter

  1. Enter a value.
  2. Select the source unit and the target unit.
  3. Read the converted result.
  4. Change units or values to convert again.

How it works

Values are converted through a base unit (such as meters) using standard conversion factors, then expressed in the target unit.

Useful for travel, construction estimates, science homework and product dimensions.

Examples

  • 1 mile ≈ 1.609 kilometers.
  • 180 cm ≈ 5 feet 11 inches (when broken down by the tool).
  • 10 inches ≈ 25.4 centimeters.

Length Converter FAQ

Are conversions exact?

They use standard factors; display may round for readability.
