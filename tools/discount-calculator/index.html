Discount Calculator

Find the final sale price after a discount and see how much you save. Free online discount calculator for percentages or fixed amounts.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server.

Advertisement

How to use the Discount Calculator

  1. Enter the original price.
  2. Enter the discount as a percentage or fixed amount (depending on the fields shown).
  3. Press Calculate to see the discounted price and savings.
  4. Copy the result or reset for another item.

How it works

A percentage discount multiplies the original price by (1 − discount/100). A fixed discount subtracts that amount from the original. The tool shows both the final price and the amount saved.

Handy for shopping, invoices, coupons and comparing deals.

Examples

  • Item $80 with 25% off → final $60, save $20.
  • Item $150 with $30 off → final $120, save $30.
  • Item $49.99 with 10% off → final about $44.99.

Discount Calculator FAQ

Can I use both percentage and fixed discounts?

Use the fields provided on the form for the type of discount you have. Results update when you calculate.

Does it handle decimal prices?

Yes. You can enter prices with cents or other decimals.

Is the Discount Calculator free?

Yes. It runs in your browser with no account required.
