Area Converter

Convert area units: square meters, square feet, acres, hectares and more. Free online area converter.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server.

How to use the Area Converter

  1. Enter an area value.
  2. Pick the source and target units.
  3. View the converted area.
  4. Change units to compare other measurements.

How it works

Area factors are derived from length conversions squared (or from standard acre/hectare definitions).

Common for real estate, flooring, land measurement and school problems.

Examples

  • 1 acre ≈ 4046.86 square meters.
  • 1 hectare = 10,000 square meters.
  • 1 square meter ≈ 10.764 square feet.
