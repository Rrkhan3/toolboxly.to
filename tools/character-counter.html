Character Counter

Use this free online character counter to count characters, lines and bytes. It works right in your browser, with no sign-up.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server.

Advertisement

How to use the Character Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the box above.
  2. Press the action button. The Word and Character Counters update live as you type.
  3. Copy or download the result, or press Clear to start again.

How it works

It counts every Unicode character, characters without whitespace, lines and UTF-8 bytes.

Example

"Hi 👋" has 4 characters and 7 bytes.

Character Counter FAQ

Is an emoji counted as one character?

Yes, but it uses more than one byte.

Why show bytes?

Some limits, such as database fields, are measured in bytes rather than characters.

Is the Character Counter free to use?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and runs in your browser.
