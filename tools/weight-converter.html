Weight Converter

Use this free online weight converter to convert between weight units. It works right in your browser, with no sign-up.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server.

Advertisement

How to use the Weight Converter

  1. Choose the unit to convert from and enter the value.
  2. Choose the unit to convert to and press Convert. Use Swap Units to reverse them.
  3. Copy the result, or press Reset to start again.

How it works

Values are converted to kilograms and then to the target unit using standard conversion factors.

Example

1 kg is about 2.2046 lb.

Weight Converter FAQ

Weight or mass?

The tool converts mass units, commonly called weight in everyday use.

Which units are supported?

Milligrams, grams, kilograms, metric tons, ounces, pounds and stone.

Is the Weight Converter free to use?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and runs in your browser.
