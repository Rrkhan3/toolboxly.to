Time Calculator

Add or subtract hours, minutes and seconds. Calculate total time duration with this free online time calculator.

Everything runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server.

How to use the Time Calculator

  1. Enter the time values you want to add or subtract.
  2. Choose the operation if the form offers add/subtract modes.
  3. Press Calculate to see the total duration.
  4. Copy the result or reset for another calculation.

How it works

Times are converted to a common unit (usually seconds), added or subtracted, then converted back to hours, minutes and seconds with proper carry-over (e.g. 75 minutes becomes 1 hour 15 minutes).

Helpful for timesheets, workout logs, video lengths and scheduling.

Examples

  • 2h 30m + 1h 45m → 4h 15m.
  • 5h 00m − 1h 20m → 3h 40m.
  • 90 minutes → 1 hour 30 minutes when normalized.

Time Calculator FAQ

Does it handle more than 24 hours?

Yes. Enter each component in the fields provided.
